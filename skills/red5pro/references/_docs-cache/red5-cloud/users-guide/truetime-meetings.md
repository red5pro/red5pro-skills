## Overview

TrueTime Meetings™ is a powerful application designed for real-time collaboration and video conferencing. This guide will walk you through the process of deploying and starting your first TrueTime Meeting.

## Accessing TrueTime Apps

To begin, log in to your Red5 Cloud account. Once logged in, navigate to the **TrueTime Apps** page from the sidebar menu on the left.

![TrueTime Apps Sidebar](/_images/red5-cloud/users-guide/tt-meetings-1.png)

## Selecting TrueTime Meetings™

On the TrueTime Apps page, you will see various application cards. Locate and click on the **TrueTime Meetings™** card.

![TrueTime Meetings™ Card](/_images/red5-cloud/users-guide/tt-meetings-2.png)

## Deploying the Application

After selecting the application, click on the **Deploy** button to begin the setup process.

![Deploy TrueTime Meetings™](/_images/red5-cloud/users-guide/tt-meetings-3.png)

## Customizing and Launching

You can customize the application settings as per your requirements. Once you have finished customizing the app, click on the **Go** button to launch it.

![Launch TrueTime Meetings™](/_images/red5-cloud/users-guide/tt-meetings-4.png)

## Starting a New Meeting

Now that your TrueTime Meetings™ application is ready, you can start a new meeting. Click on the **New Meeting** button.

![New Meeting Button](/_images/red5-cloud/users-guide/tt-meetings-5.png)

## Meeting Setup

Before joining the meeting, enter your name and select your preferred camera and microphone settings.

![Meeting Setup](/_images/red5-cloud/users-guide/tt-meetings-6.png)

## Enjoy Your Meeting

Once your setup is complete, you can join and enjoy your TrueTime Meetings™!

*(Note: Use the interface to manage participants, share your screen, and collaborate in real-time.)*
