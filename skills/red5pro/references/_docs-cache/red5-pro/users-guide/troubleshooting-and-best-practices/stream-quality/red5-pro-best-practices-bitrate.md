# Recommended Bitrates per Resolution

The following recommendations correspond with a standard framerate of 24 or 30fps.

| Resolution | Maximum (kbps) | Recommended (kbps) | Minimum (kbps) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 240p (426x240) | 700 | **400** | 300 |
| 360p (640x360) | 1000 | **750** | 400 |
| 480p (854x480) | 2000 | **1000** | 500 |
| 720p (1280x720) | 4000 | **2500** | 1500 |
| 1080p (1920x1080) | 6000 | **4500** | 3000 |

For 2K or 4K streaming with 30fps:

| Resolution | Recommended (Mbps) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1440p or "2K" (2560x1440) | **6 to 10mbps**|
| 4K (3849x2160) | **10-25mbps** |

For 2K or 4K streaming with 60fps:

| Resolution | Recommended (Mbps) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1440p or "2K" (2560x1440) | **16mbps**|
| 4K (3849x2160) | **35-45mbps** |


> Note: if you broadcast a high-resolution stream with a low bitrate and/or framerate, then the quality of the stream will drop significantly, and some browsers may resize the subscriber video.<br/>
