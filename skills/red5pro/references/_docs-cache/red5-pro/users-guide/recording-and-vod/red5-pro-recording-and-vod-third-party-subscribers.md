You can also use a desktop player such as [VLC](http://www.videolan.org/vlc/index.html) to subscribe to a recorded file. VLC can subscribe to FLV, MP4, and HLS video on demand.

With VLC media player, Open Media

![finished](/_images/protocols/vodwithred5pro/openmedia.png)

Go to the Network Tab, and type in the URL of your stream (for example, `http://127.0.0.0:5080/vod/streams/stream2.flv`)

![finished](/_images/protocols/vodwithred5pro/networkvlc.png)

then press open to view.

![finished](/_images/protocols/vodwithred5pro/playvlc.png)
